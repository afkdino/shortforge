ShortForge is a desktop app (Windows) for content creators to edit videos from their own livestreams and publish them to their own accounts. By connecting an account to ShortForge, you agree to these terms.

Using the app

  • The app only publishes videos to accounts the user connected.
  • Nothing is published unless the user picks the video and confirms the upload inside the app.
  • The user is responsible for the content they publish and for following each platform's rules.

Connected accounts

  • YouTube: the app uploads and schedules the chosen videos, reads the channel's name and numbers, and reads YouTube Analytics reports for the strategy assistant.
  • TikTok: the app reads basic profile information and posts the chosen videos to the profile, with the privacy level, interactions (comments, Duet, Stitch) and commercial content disclosure the user picks at posting time. If the account did not authorize direct posting, the video goes to drafts, where the user finishes it in the TikTok app.
  • Instagram: the app reads basic account information and publishes the chosen Reels.

Use of these accounts is also subject to each platform's terms: YouTube Terms of Service, TikTok Terms of Service and Instagram Terms of Use.

AI features

Moment suggestions, post copy and strategy suggestions are made with AI services (Google Gemini and Anthropic Claude). The user reviews everything before publishing; suggestions may contain mistakes.

Disconnecting

At any time, in the app: Channels and accounts → Sign out. Access can also be revoked in each platform's settings.

No warranty

The app is provided as is, with no guarantee of uninterrupted operation. Platforms may change or limit uploads at any time.

Privacy

What the app accesses and how data is handled is described in the Privacy Policy.
