Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026.

ShortForge is a desktop app (Windows) for content creators to edit and publish their own videos. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it uses it for and where the data is kept.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube APIs): uploading and scheduling videos (youtube.upload); reading channel and video information such as name, picture, subscribers, titles, publish dates and public counts (youtube.readonly); and reading the channel's YouTube Analytics reports such as views, retention, watch time, subscribers gained and traffic sources (yt-analytics.readonly).
  • TikTok: basic profile information such as display name, avatar and identifier (user.info.basic), and posting videos to the profile or sending them to drafts (video.publish, video.upload), only for videos the user chooses and confirms.
  • Instagram: basic account information and publishing Reels.

How the data is used

  • Uploading and scheduling the videos the user picks inside the app. Nothing is published without the user's confirmation.
  • Showing the connected accounts' name, picture and numbers on screen.
  • Giving the app's strategy assistant the channel's numbers (YouTube Analytics), to suggest what to post and when, and to compare results of earlier attempts.

The app does not use the data for advertising, does not build user profiles and does not sell it.

Where the data is kept

Access tokens, videos, transcripts and account numbers are stored only on the user's computer. ShortForge has no server and does not receive this data. YouTube metrics are refreshed every few hours while the app is open and stay on the computer until the user disconnects the account or deletes the app's folder.

Who the data is shared with

  • Video platforms: the video and the post's text go to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram at the moment of an upload the user confirmed.
  • AI services: when the user uses an AI feature, only what that feature needs is sent to the AI service the app uses, under the user's own account: Google Gemini (video excerpts and frames, to edit the moments) and Anthropic Claude (transcript, post text and channel numbers, to write copy and suggest strategy). Audio transcription and part of the analysis run on the computer itself.

No other third party receives user data.

Removing access and data

In the app: Channels and accounts → Sign out. The app deletes the stored login for that account. Access can also be revoked at any time in the permissions of the Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions), TikTok and Instagram. To delete stored videos, transcripts and metrics, delete the app's working folder on the computer. Requests or questions can be sent to the email below.

Third-party services

ShortForge uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and data use follows the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok use follows the TikTok Privacy Policy, and Instagram use, the Instagram Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

When the app starts accessing something new, this page is updated first, with the date at the top.
