Desktop app for creators

From your livestreams to shorts, without missing the best moment.

ShortForge transcribes your stream on your own computer, finds the moments worth a short, edits them in vertical format and publishes them to your YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts at the time you choose.

Features

Everything from the raw stream to the post

One flow, inside one app: open the recording and finish with your videos scheduled.

Finds the best moments

Word-by-word transcription runs on your PC's graphics card. The AI reads the whole stream and picks the clips with a hook, a reaction and a payoff.

Shorts editor

A timeline with cuts, word-by-word captions, camera framing, effects, sounds and text. You adjust everything before exporting.

Full edited VOD

The whole stream in 16:9, without dead air and "be right back" breaks, ready for YouTube. Before encoding, the app asks where to save it.

Copy and covers per channel

Title, description, caption and hashtags in each channel's tone, with a cover taken from a video frame or uploaded by you.

Scheduling and publishing

Spreads posts across days at each channel's time. YouTube schedules natively; TikTok and Instagram go out at the scheduled time.

Strategy assistant

Talks through what to post and when, looking at your channel's real YouTube numbers, and logs what was tested so you can compare later.

How it works

Four steps, you in control of each one

Import the stream

Pick the recording file or paste the VOD link. You can skip the beginning (the "starting soon" screen).

Choose the moments

The app suggests clips with a score and a reason. You decide which become shorts and fine-tune them in the editor.

Write copy and covers

Review the text written for each channel and network, change the cover and mark it as reviewed.

Schedule and publish

Set date and time and confirm. Every upload shows its progress and the link to the published video.

Integrations

What the app does with your accounts

You connect your own accounts. The app only uses the permissions below, and only when you tell it to.

YouTube

youtube.uploadyoutube.readonlyyt-analytics.readonly
  • Uploads and schedules the videos you picked, with title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • Shows your channel's name, picture and numbers.
  • Reads your videos' metrics (views, retention, watch time) for the strategy assistant.

TikTok

user.info.basicvideo.uploadvideo.publish
  • Shows the connected account's name and avatar before every post.
  • Posts the chosen video to your profile with the privacy level you select at posting time (nothing pre-selected), the interactions you allow (comments, Duet, Stitch) and the commercial content disclosure.
  • Or sends it to your drafts, so you finish it in the TikTok app.

Before posting

The app queries your account's options on TikTok, shows a preview of the video and only uploads after you confirm. After upload, it may take a few minutes for the post to appear on your profile.

Instagram

  • Shows the account's name and picture.
  • Publishes the videos you picked as Reels, at the time you scheduled.
Your data

Everything stays on your computer

ShortForge has no server and no accounts of its own. Details in the Privacy Policy.

  • Videos, logins and channel numbers are stored only on your PC.
  • Videos only go to the platforms at the moment of an upload you confirmed.
  • When you use an AI feature, only what it needs (the transcript excerpt, video frames or channel numbers) is sent to the AI service in use (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude).
  • Disconnecting takes one click: Channels and accounts → Sign out. The app deletes the stored login.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does the app post anything on its own?

No. Every post goes through your confirmation. For scheduled posts, you confirm when scheduling and the upload happens at the set time.

Do my videos go to a ShortForge server?

There is no ShortForge server. The video goes straight from your computer to YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows, with an NVIDIA graphics card for fast transcription and encoding.

Can I choose the video's privacy on TikTok?

Yes, and it's required: the app shows the options your account allows and pre-selects none.

How do I remove the app's access to my accounts?

In the app, under Channels and accounts → Sign out. You can also revoke it in the security settings of Google, TikTok and Instagram.
